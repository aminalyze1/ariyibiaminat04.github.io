Hi, I'm Amina — a data analyst and business intelligence enthusiast who enjoys turning messy information into something people can actually understand and use.

I’m particularly interested in the space where data meets real business problems. I like looking beyond the numbers to ask: What is actually happening here? Why is it happening? And what can we do differently?

My work involves analysing data, building dashboards, creating reporting systems, and developing simple structures that help businesses keep better records and make more informed decisions. I work with tools like Excel, SQL, Power BI, Python, and Google Sheets, continually expanding my toolkit as I grow.

One area I’m especially passionate about is helping small and growing businesses make sense of their operations. Many businesses have valuable data sitting in notebooks, spreadsheets, receipts, or nowhere at all. I’m interested in helping turn that information into clarity, insight, structure, and ultimately, growth.

At the same time, I’m not limited to one type of business or industry. I’m building my experience across different analytical problems, with a particular interest in business intelligence, performance analysis, operations, and the hospitality industry.

I’m naturally curious, a little obsessed with figuring out why things happen, and probably more excited about a well-organised spreadsheet than I should be.

Outside of data, I’m a student, a creative at heart, and a certified cat lover.

I’m currently building, learning, analysing, and looking for opportunities to work on problems where good data can lead to better decisions.
