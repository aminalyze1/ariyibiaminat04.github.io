    • A little about me

    I'm Amina, a Hospitality and Tourism student building my career at the intersection of data, business, and problem-solving.

    My interest in data started with curiosity. I like understanding why things happen, identifying patterns, and figuring out what can be done differently. Data analysis gave me a way to turn that curiosity into something practical.

    My academic background in hospitality has also shaped the way I approach data. I'm interested not only in the numbers themselves, but in the people, processes, operations, and business decisions behind them.

    Today, I work with tools such as Excel, SQL, Power BI, Python, and Tableau to analyse data, build dashboards, organise business information, and communicate insights.

    I'm particularly interested in helping businesses- including small and growing businesses—move from guesswork to structured, evidence-based decisions, and large enterprises use data to optimize operations, investigate problems and back decision-making.

    Outside of all things data, I'm a university student, a creative thinker, a future hospitality professional, and a person who genuinely believes cats deserve more attention than they currently receive.

    What I do

    Data Analysis

    I clean, explore and analyse datasets to uncover patterns, trends, relationships and opportunities.

    Business Intelligence

    I turn business data into meaningful reports and dashboards that make performance easier to understand.

    Business Performance Analysis

    I investigate sales, profitability, operations and other performance metrics to understand what is happening and where improvement may be possible.

    Data Visualisation

    I present complex information through clear, intuitive dashboards and visual reports that make insights easier to communicate.
